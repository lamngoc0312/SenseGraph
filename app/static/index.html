How to Use SenseGraph

  • Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between buttons, and Enter or Space to activate them.
  • Screen reader users (NVDA / VoiceOver): Leave browser voice off to avoid duplicate speech. Every step is announced automatically.
  • No screen reader? Click Start spoken guidance in the top audio bar for calm spoken narration.
  • Switch to Educator / Advanced anytime in the header to upload images, review AI proposals, or use the Evidence-Grounded Tutor.
